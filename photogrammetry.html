Subsea photogrammetry

Advanced subsea photogrammetry and 3D inspection: high-resolution subsea imaging with specialised 3D modelling, giving detailed digital representations of pipelines, subsea structures and seabed environments.

What we deliver

Seven inspection and survey services built on the same 3D data.

  • 3D subsea modelling

    Creating detailed models of pipelines, manifolds, structures and seabed features.

  • Pipeline inspection

    Assessing pipeline geometry, damage, displacement and exposed sections.

  • Subsea metrology

    Taking dimensional measurements to support the design and installation of subsea connections.

  • Structural integrity assessment

    Documenting visible defects, corrosion damage and structural deformation.

  • Decommissioning surveys

    Assessing infrastructure before dismantling, cutting or removal.

  • As-built surveys

    Verifying installed equipment against engineering designs.

  • Digital asset monitoring

    Comparing 3D surveys over time to identify changes in subsea infrastructure.

The outcome

Practical, reliable data that supports better engineering decisions, reduces unnecessary offshore intervention and improves project efficiency, safety and cost effectiveness.
